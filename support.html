Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Open the Available Tasks page, choose a task that matches your skills, review the task requirements, and select Start Task. The task workspace will be connected when the backend system is completed.
After a submitted task is reviewed and approved, the applicable reward will be added to your account balance. The real approval and payment system will be connected through the backend later.
Go to the Withdraw section from your dashboard. You will be able to select an available payment method, enter the required information, and submit a withdrawal request.
Share your referral link with people you invite to Taskora. Referral tracking and reward rules will be connected to the backend system after Firebase integration.
Use the password recovery option on the Login page. The actual password recovery system will be connected when Firebase authentication is added.
You can submit a support request using the form on this page. Your ticket will be connected to the real support system after the backend is implemented.
